S1Sort (Seiri)
Audit Criteria Audit Question Score (0–4) Finding / Action Required
Items not needed for current work are removed or red-tagged Are there any items in the area that are not needed for current work?
Only the correct tools, materials, and documents are present Is every item here actually needed and in the right quantity?
No excess inventory or obsolete items in the work area Is there any excess stock, expired material, or broken equipment present?
Red tag area exists and is managed — items cleared within 48 hrs Is the red tag process active and followed?
Decision criteria for needed vs not-needed is known by all Can every team member explain what belongs here and what doesn't?
S2Set in Order (Seiton)
Audit Criteria Audit Question Score (0–4) Finding / Action Required
Every item has a clearly defined, labelled storage location Does everything have a home? Is every home labelled?
Items are returned to their location after every use Are items consistently found in their designated places?
Visual controls (shadow boards, labels, floor lines) are in place Can anyone find anything without asking?
Frequently used items are within arm's reach; rarely used items further away Is the layout ergonomic and logical?
Safety and emergency equipment is immediately visible and accessible Can safety/emergency items be found within 10 seconds?
S3Shine (Seiso)
Audit Criteria Audit Question Score (0–4) Finding / Action Required
Work area, floors, and surfaces are clean and free of contamination Is the area clean enough to detect abnormalities?
Equipment is inspected during cleaning — defects noted and actioned Is cleaning used as an inspection activity?
Sources of contamination (leaks, spills, dust) are identified and fixed Are causes of contamination being addressed at source?
Cleaning is part of the daily routine, not an occasional event Is there evidence of daily cleaning habits?
Cleaning tools and supplies are accessible and properly stored Are cleaning materials in good condition and easy to find?
S4Standardise (Seiketsu)
Audit Criteria Audit Question Score (0–4) Finding / Action Required
Visual standards are documented and displayed at the point of use Are photo standards or visual boards posted and current?
5S responsibilities are clearly assigned — who does what, by when Is there a cleaning/inspection rota with named owners?
All 3 prior S's are maintained consistently across ALL shifts Does the area look the same on night shift as day shift?
Deviation from the standard is immediately visible (abnormal is obvious) Is it obvious when something is out of place?
Cleaning and inspection schedules are posted and followed Are completed schedules visible with dates and signatures?
S5Sustain (Shitsuke)
Audit Criteria Audit Question Score (0–4) Finding / Action Required
5S audit is conducted at the defined frequency — results posted Are audit results displayed and visible to the team?
All team members follow 5S standards without supervision Is 5S a habit, not a performance for the auditor?
All action items from the previous audit are implemented Are previous findings closed before the next audit?
5S performance is a KPI reviewed at daily/weekly team meetings Is 5S part of the management system — not a separate activity?
5S discipline is improving or sustaining over time Does the trend show improvement or stability?
